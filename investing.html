Inès-Rose Masson Investing Life

My path into venture has mostly been driven by curiosity. I studied finance and technology in Paris, then used investing as a way to keep entering worlds I knew little about, from software and climate to deep tech and industry. Over time, I became increasingly drawn to the companies where technology meets the physical world. That curiosity brought me to London and to Transition, where I now back early-stage founders using AI and technology to rethink the systems, industries and infrastructure around us.
